02Pure Binaural Beats & Hemispheric Synchronization

Binaural beats represent a well-documented auditory illusion occurring in the brain when two slightly different carrier frequencies are presented to each ear. The brain processes these signals in the superior olivary nucleus, creating a perceived third frequency representing the difference (e.g., L: 136.1 Hz, R: 140.1 Hz produces a 4 Hz Theta beat).

This difference frequency entrains the brain's electrical oscillations (electroencephalographic waves) through the Frequency Following Response (FFR), inducing specified cognitive states such as Theta (4 Hz for meditation/deep absorption), Alpha (10 Hz for focus), or Delta (2 Hz for recovery).

Technical Implementation: Neurocode Studio synthesizes pure mathematical sine waves without harmonic overtones. 03Psychoacoustic Masking & Surgical Notch EQ

In classical psychoacoustics, frequency masking occurs when a louder, broad-spectrum sound (such as background music or noise) covers or makes inaudible a quieter, narrow-band sound (such as the binaural carrier tones). This is dictated by the "critical bands" of the human cochlea, as defined in Eberhard Zwicker's critical band model.

To keep the therapeutic binaural beats fully functional without forcing the user to raise their volume (which degrades the listening experience), the background music must be surgically altered to prevent masking.

Technical Implementation: When binaural beats and background music are combined, the DSP engine automatically applies a digital Infinite Impulse Response (IIR) notch filter with an extremely high quality factor (Q = 30). This carves narrow V-shaped frequency cuts in the background music exactly at 136.1 Hz and 140.1 Hz. The cut is so surgical (~4 Hz wide) that the human ear cannot perceive any change in the music, yet it opens an "acoustic window" that makes the binaural beat highly effective.
