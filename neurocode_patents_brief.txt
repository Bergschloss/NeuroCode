1. AMPLITUDE MODULATION (AM) SUBLIMINAL ENCODING (SILENT SUBLIMINALS)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
* Technology Overview:
  This technology transfers the speaker's vocal signal (which naturally resides 
  within the 100 Hz to 4,000 Hz range) into the high-frequency near-ultrasonic 
  spectrum (ranging from 3,000 Hz up to 18,000 Hz) using Amplitude Modulation (AM) 
  onto a grid of carrier frequencies. This process shifts the vocal signal into 
  the upper limit of human hearing, where conscious auditory perception drops to 
  near zero, yet the subconscious mind continues to register and decode the 
  envelope of the modulated wave.

* Technical DSP Implementation:
  The program generates between 4 and 32 parallel layers of affirmations. The 
  carrier frequencies are distributed linearly across the high-frequency 
  spectrum. Shifting the signal to the near-ultrasound range completely 
  eliminates conscious parsing of the words. A dynamic Time-Stretch ranging from 
  2.0x to 10.0x is applied to each layer to make the speech completely 
  unrecognizable consciously, while remaining easily parsed by the subconscious. 
  "Hard Stereo" panning is enforced, routing even-numbered layers to the Left 
  channel and odd-numbered layers to the Right channel (up to 16 unique layers 
  per ear).

2. PURE BINAURAL BEATS & HEMISPHERIC SYNCHRONIZATION (FREQUENCY FOLLOWING RESPONSE)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
* Technology Overview:
  Binaural beats are an auditory illusion created by the brain when two pure sine 
  waves with slightly different frequencies are played separately into each ear 
  (e.g., 136.1 Hz in the left ear and 140.1 Hz in the right ear). The brain 
  combines these signals in the superior olivary nucleus of the brainstem, 
  producing a perceived third frequency representing the difference (in this 
  case, a 4 Hz Theta beat). This difference frequency entrains brainwave activity 
  via the Frequency Following Response (FFR), synchronizing the electrical 
  activity of the left and right hemispheres.

* Technical DSP Implementation:
  Neurocode Studio synthesizes pure binaural beats using mathematically precise 
  sine generators without harmonic overtones. The left channel uses a fundamental 
  carrier of 136.1 Hz (C# / Ohm frequency), while the right channel uses the 
  offset. Supported target states include Delta (2 Hz for sleep/recovery), Theta 
  (4 Hz for deep meditation), Alpha (10 Hz for focus/learning), and Beta (15 Hz 
  for active processing).

* Patent References:

  - U.S. Patent No. 5,213,562 (Robert A. Monroe, 1993 - Expired): Method of

3. SURGICAL NOTCH EQ (PREVENTING PSYCHOACOUSTIC FREQUENCY MASKING)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
* Technology Overview:
  In psychoacoustics, frequency masking occurs when a louder sound (like music or 
  pink noise) covers a quieter sound within the same frequency band. This is 
  governed by the critical bands of the human cochlea (Eberhard Zwicker's critical 
  band model). When background music is played alongside a binaural beat, the 
  music frequencies mask the binaural carriers, reducing their therapeutic 
  efficacy.

* Technical DSP Implementation:
  To keep binaural beats effective without raising their volume (which would 
  disturb the user), the DSP engine applies a digital Infinite Impulse Response 
  (IIR) notch filter with an extremely high quality factor (Q = 30). This carves 
  narrow V-shaped frequency cuts in the background music exactly at the carrier 
  frequencies (136.1 Hz and 140.1 Hz). Because the notch is extremely narrow 
  Clicking at (~4-5 Hz wide), the listener does not perceive any degradation in 
  the music, yet it opens a clear frequency window for the binaural beat to operate.

* Patent Reference Context:
  Notch filtering music is widely used in Tailor-Made Notched Music Training 
  (TMNMT) for tonal tinnitus management via lateral cortical inhibition. 
  Specific commercial hearing-aid implementations are patented by auditory tech 
  leaders (e.g., Sivantos / Signia Notch Therapy, licensed under U.S. Patent No. 
  9,549,269 B2).
